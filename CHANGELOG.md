# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-12

- Introduces Aster Flow: a frameless, searchable command rail with a charcoal fade and muted orange accent, usable in gameplay and menus.
- Ships Auto Sprint, Fullbright, hold-to-zoom, Coordinates, Performance, and Keystrokes with inline settings and per-profile toggle keys.
- Adds named profile snapshots, rename, confirmed deletion, and confirmed reset. Profiles preserve independent states, settings, and shortcuts.
- Supports mouse and keyboard navigation, filtered results, focus shortcuts, scrolling, and compact windows.
- Preserves menu parents and singleplayer pause state. Hides the crosshair while Flow is open and respects Minecraft's key-binding capture.
- Validates imported settings and duplicate bindings, writes settings atomically, retains corrupt-file backups, and protects newer config formats.
- Includes MIT-licensed sources, font license notices, installation and troubleshooting guides, reproducible archive settings, SHA-256 checksums, and CI.

The earlier 0.1.0 workspace builds were development previews.
